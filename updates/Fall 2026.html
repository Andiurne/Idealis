Fall News

Greetings!

In preparation for some people to possibly discover this site, I felt it right to post an update you don't need to know me personally to receive.

Unfortunately, while I hoped out loud in my April news that I would have some form of short story as prequel to Indelible worked on, that hasn't come to fruition at the moment. However, come to the end of the year, I will have three individual pieces of Aliffrüme writing available.

New and Scheduled Writings

  1. "One Last Letter," a submission to Shiaides' flash fiction contest, accessible today.
  2. "Old Soil," a short story detailing the origin of Soël, a vital general in the Jïumannian Revolutionary War.
  3. A mystery even to myself, at the moment.
  4. "PHLG 221: Intro to Modern Phlogistics," a diegetic lecture series explaining the system of phlogistics in a linear, university-like fashion.

Update Schedule

I implied in the April update that such news would be monthly. This has proven to be unfeasible, so unless there ends up being a need or demand for otherwise, I'll allow myself to update here only when necessary.

Encyclopedia

While a major diff to the encyclopedia is being pushed with this article, the text itself is still self-contradicting or overly dry in many places (as a work 6 years under the microscope only intended to be internal tends to be...), especially with regards to the phlogistics. Fixing this will be a long process that takes a back seat to narrative projects, but is something I hope to remedy nonetheless.

Newcomers & Contact

To anybody visiting the site for the first time, if you'd like to get in contact with myself and others to discuss my work, please feel free to join us on the Discord! I welcome questions and earnest critique!

Ad idealis, Andiurne
