Hawen couldn't remember the last time&nbsp;
his desk had been this clean. The letter&nbsp;
cut a staunch white picture on the mahogany,&nbsp;
and the inky black of his lettering&nbsp;
caught the last rays of sunset through&nbsp;
the window. His garnet pen rested neatly&nbsp;
to the side, just beneath an ornate&nbsp;
ink well finally run dry.&nbsp;
His lecture notes sat neatly organized&nbsp;
in shelves beneath the window,&nbsp;
meticulously catalogued by subject&nbsp;
in the hours he'd spent since sealing the&nbsp;
parchment. They had previously found&nbsp;
their home just above everywhere in the&nbsp;
room besides the desk, and Hawen had&nbsp;
no doubt they'd return within days.&nbsp;
But she would complain, rightly,&nbsp;
if they remained there too long.&nbsp;

Hawen turned to open their closet,&nbsp;
running his fingers over the dusty cotton&nbsp;
of her sweaters before settling on the&nbsp;
rough wool of his greatcoat&nbsp;
and tossing it over his shoulders.&nbsp;
In a swift, choreographed motion,&nbsp;
he swayed across the room back to his desk&nbsp;
and slid the letter into his breast pocket,&nbsp;
nearly stepping out the door before he caught&nbsp;
himself at the threshold. He almost forgot.&nbsp;

"Until the evening, love."&nbsp;

> *Gyllicadajë Monefeorforma, Autumn 1009*\
>\
> *To my dear Roädea,*\
>\
> *It's been some weeks since I last wrote to you,*&nbsp;
*and for that I sincerely apologize.*&nbsp;
*Preparing Hütha for university has taken up*&nbsp;
*no small amount of my time. Your colleagues in*&nbsp;
*the arterial college are excited to welcome her;*&nbsp;
*I believe they have every faith she'll follow in your*&nbsp;
*footsteps. I myself am inclined to believe them.*&nbsp;
*She still holds that same passion that first*&nbsp;
*drew me to you, not only for the workings of the body,*&nbsp;
*but for helping the souls that inhabit them.*&nbsp;
*She'll make a fine doctor.*&nbsp;

The soft crunch of leaves on pavement&nbsp;
accompanied the clack of Hawen's heels&nbsp;
as he made his way up the stone-paved streets&nbsp;
of Haavenküth, raising a hand to shield his eyes&nbsp;
as he savored the occasional glimpse&nbsp;
of gilded amber light reflecting off the westward bay&nbsp;
betwixt brick-and-mortar townhouses&nbsp;
as the Sun slowly left his sight.&nbsp;
It would be gone in a few short hours.&nbsp;
Hardly any time at all.&nbsp;

> *The ink you gave me before departing runs*&nbsp;
*dry as I write this. Though I'm sure the pen*&nbsp;
*will occupy my desk a while longer; your judgment*&nbsp;
*served you well in commissioning it from Alferhthiuer.*&nbsp;
*The flashing streaks of red remind me of your eyes,*&nbsp;
*and help me to keep their image in my mind as*&nbsp;
*your journey lengthens. Hütha holds the scarf you*&nbsp;
*left for her in similar regard; I expect she'll*&nbsp;
*be wearing it during the inauguration, despite*&nbsp;
*the dean's best attempt at "preserving the sanctity*&nbsp;
*of uniform." I'll find a way to convince them.*&nbsp;

As the buildings reached lower and grew sparse&nbsp;
along the sidewalk, Hawen found himself leaving&nbsp;
the city for the hilltop forests dyed in brown&nbsp;
and gold. The skyline lay entirely beneath him,&nbsp;
attached firmly to the ground as he seemed&nbsp;
to rise above the evening fog. Terracotta rooftops&nbsp;
painted the hillside, divided by the lilac of&nbsp;
everviolet trees and the banners modeled after&nbsp;
their color, flown in recognition of the autumnal equinox,&nbsp;
Remembrance Day.&nbsp;

> *I swear, as time goes by, I see more of you*&nbsp;
*in her. It shouldn't scare me as much as it does.*&nbsp;
*I know you. I know you're doing good, no matter where*&nbsp;
*you might be right now. It never mattered who it was,*&nbsp;
*nor when, nor where. You could never stand to see someone*&nbsp;
*suffer, for even a single second. Hütha carries that*&nbsp;
*spark in her too. She claims it what draws her to the*&nbsp;
*field, and I don't think it's a lie.*&nbsp;
*But I do know part of her chases it because*&nbsp;
*it brings her closer to you, in a way.*&nbsp;
*But that's just a father's intuition.*&nbsp;

As he crested the top of the hill, the full majesty of&nbsp;
Witafjürgen University came into view. The hues of&nbsp;
sunset painted its marble walls like a brush upon&nbsp;
canvas, while its observatory spires reached upward&nbsp;
to scrape the clouds as the hand of a child would&nbsp;
reach off the side of a canoe to briefly touch&nbsp;
the ocean's awesome splendor.&nbsp;
It was that natural awe&nbsp;
for the world which had inhabited Roädea and him,&nbsp;
and drew them together in their studies here.&nbsp;
The same wonder that inhabited their daughter.&nbsp;
The same wonder that led him to teach here,&nbsp;
hoping to cultivate it in the next round of&nbsp;
aspirant firekeepers, before it was inevitably&nbsp;
snuffed out by the assurance of an old man that&nbsp;
he'd seen everything worth seeing.&nbsp;
But for today, at least,&nbsp;
his attention was drawn to a well-maintained&nbsp;
garden just outside the walls.&nbsp;

> *When Hütha first learned of these letters,*&nbsp;
*I made a promise to myself and to her*&nbsp;
*that the ink well would determine their end.*&nbsp;
*I thought better of refilling it after the first month.*&nbsp;
*So this shall be the final time I write to you.*&nbsp;

Even in fall, some flowers still bloomed.&nbsp;
Hawen admired their strength in the face of the&nbsp;
inevitable as he stood before the stone.&nbsp;

> *Were you here, I know you would insist I hope*&nbsp;
*it be a long while before we meet again.*&nbsp;
*Though a part of me wishes otherwise.*&nbsp;
*Selfish, I know. It's become quieter lately.*&nbsp;


> *I'm here because it's what you would want.*&nbsp;
> *I'm here for Hütha.*&nbsp;
> *In time, I hope, I'll be here for myself too.*\
>\
> *Your love eternal,*\
>\
> *Hawen*

Setting it gently beneath the headstone,&nbsp;
Hawen gazed at the mixture of finality&nbsp;
and memory for a brief moment.&nbsp;
Then, with his mind made up,&nbsp;
Hawen set the envelope alight in offering&nbsp;
with a compressed bolt of plasma&nbsp;
from his gloved hand, before&nbsp;
setting back off towards town to meet&nbsp;
Hütha for supper. The inscription,&nbsp;
kept clean through the seasons,&nbsp;
read succinctly:&nbsp;

::: center
*Roädea Liffhälend-Sijëlwürta*

*Autumn 983 - Spring 1009*

*Beloved Mother*

*Respected Scholar*

*Valiant Medic*
:::
