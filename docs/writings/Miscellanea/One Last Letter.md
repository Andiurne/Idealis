Hawen couldn't remember the last time
his desk had been this clean. The letter
cut a staunch white picture on the mahogany,
and the inky black of his lettering
caught the last rays of sunset through
the window. His garnet pen rested neatly
to the side, just beneath an ornate
ink well finally run dry.
His lecture notes sat neatly organized
in shelves beneath the window,
meticulously catalogued by subject
in the hours he'd spent since sealing the
parchment. They had previously found
their home just above everywhere in the
room besides the desk, and Hawen had
no doubt they'd return within days.
But she would complain, rightly,
if they remained there too long.

Hawen turned to open their closet,
running his fingers over the dusty cotton
of her sweaters before settling on the
rough wool of his greatcoat
and tossing it over his shoulders.
In a swift, choreographed motion,
he swayed across the room back to his desk
and slid the letter into his breast pocket,
nearly stepping out the door before he caught
himself at the threshold. He almost forgot.

"Until the evening, love."


<blockquote>
<p><em>Gyllicadajë Monefeorforma, Autumn 1009</em><br>
<br>
<em>To my dear Roädea,</em><br>
<br>
<em>It’s been some weeks since I last wrote to you, </em>
<em>and for that I sincerely apologize. </em>
<em>Preparing Hütha for university has taken up </em>
<em>no small amount of my time. Your colleagues in </em>
<em>the arterial college are excited to welcome her; </em>
<em>I believe they have every faith she’ll follow in your </em>
<em>footsteps. I myself am inclined to believe them. </em>
<em>She still holds that same passion that first </em>
<em>drew me to you, not only for the workings of the body, </em>
<em>but for helping the souls that inhabit them. </em>
<em>She’ll make a fine doctor. </em>
</p>
</blockquote>

The soft crunch of leaves on pavement
accompanied the clack of Hawen's heels
as he made his way up the stone-paved streets
of Haavenküth, raising a hand to shield his eyes
as he savored the occasional glimpse
of gilded amber light reflecting off the westward bay
betwixt brick-and-mortar townhouses
as the Sun slowly left his sight.
It would be gone in a few short hours.
Hardly any time at all.

<blockquote><p>
<em>The ink you gave me before departing runs </em>
<em>dry as I write this. Though I'm sure the pen </em>
<em>will occupy my desk a while longer; your judgment </em>
<em>served you well in commissioning it from Alferhthiuer. </em>
<em>The flashing streaks of red remind me of your eyes, </em>
<em>and help me to keep their image in my mind as </em>
<em>your journey lengthens. Hütha holds the scarf you </em>
<em>left for her in similar regard; I expect she'll </em>
<em>be wearing it during the inauguration, despite </em>
<em>the dean's best attempt at "preserving the sanctity </em>
<em>of uniform." I'll find a way to convince them. </em>
</p></blockquote>

As the buildings reached lower and grew sparse
along the sidewalk, Hawen found himself leaving
the city for the hilltop forests dyed in brown
and gold. The skyline lay entirely beneath him,
attached firmly to the ground as he seemed
to rise above the evening fog. Terracotta rooftops
painted the hillside, divided by the lilac of
everviolet trees and the banners modeled after
their color, flown in recognition of the autumnal equinox:
Remembrance Day.

<blockquote><p>
<em>I swear, as time goes by, I see more of you </em>
<em>in her. It shouldn't scare me as much as it does. </em>
<em>I know you. I know you're doing good, no matter where </em>
<em>you might be right now. It never mattered who it was, </em>
<em>nor when, nor where. You could never stand to see someone </em>
<em>suffer, for even a single second. Hütha carries that </em>
<em>spark in her too. She claims it what draws her to the </em>
<em>field, and I don't think it's a lie. </em>
<em>But I do know part of her chases it because </em>
<em>it brings her closer to you, in a way. </em>
<em>But that's just a father's intuition. </em>
</p>
</blockquote>

As he crested the top of the hill, the full majesty of
Witafjürgen University came into view. The hues of
sunset painted its marble walls like a brush upon
canvas, while its observatory spires reached upward
to scrape the clouds as the hand of a child would
reach off the side of a canoe to briefly touch
the ocean's awesome splendor.
It was that natural awe
for the world which had inhabited Roädea and him,
and drew them together in their studies here.
The same wonder that inhabited their daughter.
The same wonder that led him to teach here,
hoping to cultivate it in the next round of
aspirant firekeepers, before it was inevitably
snuffed out by the assurance of an old man that
he'd seen everything worth seeing.
But for today, at least,
his attention was drawn to a well-maintained
garden just outside the walls.

<blockquote><p>
<em>When Hütha first learned of these letters, </em>
<em>I made a promise to myself and to her </em>
<em>that the ink well would determine their end. </em>
<em>I thought better of refilling it after the first month. </em>
<em>So this shall be the final time I write to you. </em>
</p></blockquote>

Even in fall, some flowers still bloomed.
Hawen admired their strength in the face of the
inevitable as he stood before the stone.

<blockquote><p>
<em>Were you here, I know you would insist I hope </em>
<em>it be a long while before we meet again. </em>
<em>Though a part of me wishes otherwise. </em>
<em>Selfish, I know. It's become quieter lately. </em><br>
<br>
<em>I'm here because it's what you would want. </em>
<em>I'm here for Hütha. </em>
<em>In time, I hope, I'll be here for myself too. </em><br>`
<br>
<em>Your love eternal,</em><br>
<br>
<em>Hawen</em>
</p></blockquote>

Setting it gently beneath the headstone,
Hawen gazed at the mixture of finality
and memory for a brief moment.
Then, with his mind made up,
Hawen set the envelope alight in offering
with a compressed bolt of plasma
from his gloved hand, before
setting back off towards town to meet
Hütha for supper. The inscription,
kept clean through the seasons,
read succinctly:

::: center
*Roädea Liffhälend-Sijëlwürta*

*Autumn 983 - Spring 1009*

*Beloved Mother*

*Respected Scholar*

*Valiant Medic*
:::
