# Soulsmithing
The lesser-known assistant to many [firekeepers](../../Glossary.md#Firekeeper), soulsmiths are those who make use of infusion and its techniques to craft tools, weapons, and other metallic items largely unaided. This allows for fine precision through [melding and rending](../Soulfire/Interactions/Coupling.md#Melding%20&%20Rending), which provides control that distinguishes its products from mundane blacksmithing. In addition to convenience–especially in environments disagreeable to fire, like Arenfürich or the Tohneasian Mist-forests–the process also gives the product a higher tolerance for [infusion](../Soulfire/Interactions/Infusion.md) and a smoother response to manipulation.

Items crafted by a soulsmith are referred to as "soulforged," since they often have distinguishing qualities from those made by blacksmiths.

## Techniques
### Cold Forging
It is possible to perform soulsmithing without warming the material, in a process known as cold forging. At low temperatures, the common metals are not innately pliable, nor will it naturally form bonds with itself, meaning that all control over the structure must be intentional and deliberate. While this process consumes less soulfire by necessity, it is notoriously difficult to somehow manually disassembled and reconstruct the material deliberately at such a fine level of control.

The main benefit of cold forging, aside from its increased efficiency, is the fine control one has over the finished product. Grains and imperfections can be meticulously controlled for unparalleled durability, sharpness, strength, or any other mechanical property.
