# Soul Assertion
A technique wherein [Soulfire](../Pure%20Phlogistics/Soul/Soulfire.md) is, in a way, infused *through* the soul plane, producing results in alignment with the [Soul](../Pure%20Phlogistics/Soul/Soul.md)’s view of itself. A unique feature of [soulkeepers](../Applied%20Phlogistics/Soulkeeping.md), it serves as an invaluable method of healing from injury and illness. However, it requires a number of components to function. 

Chiefly, the user must be relatively attuned to the topology of their soul, which means an extensive knowledge of the self. Secondarily, an image of the state one wishes to assert, subconsciously or otherwise. This notably limits what can be done for chronic ailments and subtly slow transformations.

## Harmonic Assertion
Soul assertion can also be performed on a foreign target, provided they’re at least subconsciously willing. In this case, a skilled practitioner can much more easily feel an external soul, and supply them with harmonic soulfire by empathizing with their current state. Though, naturally, this will never be as powerful as if the target had learned for themselves.

### Applied to Animals
Common fauna typically have a strong enough [impression](../Pure%20Phlogistics/Conscious%20Plane/Impression.md) to maintain it after injury, thus harmonic assertion can be applied to heal them in a similar manner to sapients. The process is often simpler, as given the less refined nature of an impression, one rarely has to restrain or mutate any fundamental phases to achieve harmony.

This process is utilized heavily in meat production for soulkeeping cultures, as a "template" animal can have its meat and other parts repeatedly harvested, then regrown with harmonic assertion, which is much more efficient than rearing livestock from birth. This process is colloquially referred to as "templating," or its product as "template meat." Moreover, aside from the obvious ethical reasons, ensuring the animals are not disturbed by the process through temporary, strategic nerve severance (keeping chemical anesthetics from poisoning the meat) makes the harmonic assertion much simpler.

### Applied to Plants
Unlike fauna, common flora often do not have a strong enough impression to sustain it after injury, with the notable exception of many species of mushroom and some trees. This makes the templating process for the purpose of plant-based food production, especially grain, infeasible.

Luckily, plants *are* able to be assisted another way: while slightly different from typical harmonic assertion, a modified process allows for certain plants to be assisted in their growth cycles, a process which is currently infeasible in animals. While simple, this technique single-handedly supports the populations and export economies of many soulkeeping nations, cutting down drastically on resource consumption and time cost in farming.
