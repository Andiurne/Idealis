# Adamant
The common name for a [pneumaphilic](Pneumaphilic%20Materials.md) variety of man-made [soulforged](../../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Applied%20Phlogistics/Soulsmithing.md) carbon allotropes, crafted into various crystal structures from various sources and with varying purities. The gold standard for the titular powder in [powderblades](../../Weapons%20and%20Military%20Devices/Powderblade.md) and [powderstaves](../../Weapons%20and%20Military%20Devices/Powderstave.md), they are often separated into varying degrees of purity in regards to their crystal structure, as a higher grade enables a greater [coupling capacity](../../../Glossary.md#Coupling%20Capacity). They are also separated into color grades based on the type of and density of impurity.  

## Varieties
There are various kinds of adamant, depending upon the source of the carbon. The most common is made of bituminous coal, and which due to the various hydrogen and nitrogen impurities rather resembles [ametrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ametrine): having a lilac and sunflower yellow color. The higher grades– which are made of anthracite–are initially colorless, but take on an emerald-green color as they are continually coupled due to [spark](../../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Pure%20Phlogistics/Sparks.md)irradiation. This is one of the major reasons for the association between hues of green and [Firekeeping](../../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Applied%20Phlogistics/Firekeeping.md) in many cultures.

### Gilded Adamant
While most variants of adamant are crafted with a cubic structure like typical diamond, it is also possible, though much rarer for the skill needed, for adamant to be formed in a hexagonal structure. Such adamant is referred to as "gilded" for the characteristic yellowish-brown color of the structure, which also grants it a much harder base durability, allowing for infused gilded blades to slice through almost any material and deflect piercing blows with ease.
