# Silversteel
A [Melded Alloy](Melded%20Alloy.md) used widely in scriptlighter’s armor and weapons, known for its high coupling factor and toughness while maintaining flexibility. Taking full advantage of the unique structures allowed by melding, silver-steel is composed of a tungsten-carbide outer shell, [blacksteel](../../Glossary.md#Blacksteel) inter-layer, and tungsten backing. The TC shell helps to deflect blades and projectiles while also contributing to a high reactive combustion capacity, while the blacksteel core provides an elastic rebound for blunt weapons and easy self-repair through melding. The tungsten is also specially soulforged to have long, linear grains, helping to displace energy. When breaking, the outer shell expanding under the swell of the blacksteel also helps to soften the impact.

For these properties, silversteel is the alloy of choice for constructing anti-kinetic armor, and anything else that needs to withstand high-momentum impacts. While uncommon, some high-grade arterial weapons intended for hit-and-run tactics on larger [shades](../../Glossary.md#Shade) will also be crafted from silversteel.
