# Arteries
An organization of [soulkeeping](../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Applied%20Phlogistics/Soulkeeping.md), agricultural monks that operates primarily within Jïumannes, whose main purpose is to be a caste kept entirely separate from gimyndine in order to facilitate safe food production. This is mainly accomplished through the practice of soulkeeping, which is then utilized to secondary benefit in accelerating production, cleansing shades, tending to livestock, and other minor ends.

In order to fulfill their purpose, the Arteries practice a wide range of activities. They train in martial arts, both to discipline their bodies and to better fend off Shades encroaching on village settlements; they also train in meditation, in order to build their own soulfire and practice handling it without the assistance of engrams.  

Given their peculiar status, the group is treated like a social caste entirely separate from the hierarchies, being simultaneously valued and ignored. Common practice is to offer them due reverence, trade wares for their foodstuffs, and elsewise leave them be.

## Individual Roles
### Agriculturalists
Simply, the lifeblood of any arterial nation. Agriculturalists, or "aggies," are more or less solely responsible for staple food production, including planting, maintenance, accelerating growth with harmonic infusion, and harvesting. However, there are some agriculturalists who dedicate themselves to other plant life, such as producing [quickwoods](../../Glossary.md#Quickwood) for construction, flowers for various festivals, and miscellany plant-based textiles.

### Herdsmen
Those in charge of responsibly managing livestock and other domesticated animals. Ironically, similar to medics, they are typically required to be skilled in [harmonic assertion](../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Soulfire/Soul%20Assertion.md#Harmonic%20Assertion), along with painless methods of paralysis. While useful for caretaking, this is far more important for their other duty: meat production. This way, livestock can be kept unconscious and alive while parts of their body are harvested for meat and other purposes, so they can then be peacefully returned to pasture. 

With an alternating schedule and rapid healing, this allows for "meat donor" animals to be humanely kept as potent protein sources, while avoiding the significant time and grain costs of raising a larger volume of animals from birth. While this skill is known to harder to acquire than harmonic assertion within one's own species, particularly skilled herdsmen can even behead smaller livestock and regrow their entire bodies without major complications.

### Medic
Typically only found within pulmonaries close to cities or other gatherings of laypeople, medics are those trained in sapient biology and harmonic assertion, tasked with healing the general populace. 

### Cleanser
While the duty of cleansing shades may be taken up by any [soulkeeper](../../Glossary.md#Soulkeeper), the specific duty of a cleanser is to protect the fields from [shades](../../Glossary.md#Shade) which accrue in the deep forest. Usually, those that are particularly powerful will be able to find more gainful employment in becoming mercenaries or private security for wealthy merchants, so those that remain in the cloth are either fairly principled or fairly low in soulflow.

That being said, veteran cleansers will often offer basic combat lessons to those willing, and many a master combatant can trace their instruction back to a cleanser.

### Steelshaper
A [soulsmith](../../Glossary.md#Soulsmith) specializing in the creation of arterial tools and cleansing weapons. While they do often work with simple [blacksteel](../../Glossary.md#Blacksteel) as their name suggests, some pulmonaries which find themselves combating more dangerous shades will also task their smiths with handling [silver-steel](../../Technology/Materials/Silver-steel.md) in armor production.

> A steelshaper is distinct from a steelsmith, as it also is from a blacksmith, goldsmith, silversmith, and further. In general, a "smith" is a mundane worker of the material, while a "shaper" is one that uses firekeeping to do so. This has the notable exception of soulsmiths in general, which has been translated as such because a "soulshaper" would have a different connotation, and there is no such thing as mundane soulsmithing.

### Librarian
Given their heavy reliance on intricate [engrams](../../Intuitive%20Phenomena%20&%20Theory/Pure%20Phlogistics/Engram.md) for planting, harvesting, and processing foodstuffs, many pulmonaries will designate full-time [reckoners](../../Glossary.md#Reckoner) and [flamescribes](../../Glossary.md#Flamescribe) charged with keeping, maintaining, and developing engrams for the use of the pulmonary, along with keeping records of their production, losses, and other accounts of business.

Furthermore, the librarians will sometimes oversee literal libraries containing relevant knowledge on their duties, which will often serve double duty housing works on other subjects. Some librarians will even offer tutoring to the public in their off hours, and it's not uncommon for low-class families to send their children along this path.

### Ritualist
A catch-all term for arteries which fulfill specific, niche functions that might otherwise be contracted out to civilians, but preferentially are handled in-house for ritualistic reasons. For example, to avoid cross-contamination, Arteries prefer to have their weapons, tools, clothing, and infrastructure crafted by themselves, but such craftsmen willing to devote themselves to the cloth can be few and far between.

## Pulmonaries
A pulmonary is an outpost of Arteries, typically serving it's community in some combination of shade defense, food supply, health, and various traditional ceremonies.

### The Core
The largest pulmonary, which is led by the Lifeheart themselves; currently the position is filled by [Alfiürwit](Narratives/Characters/Birth%20of%20a%20Nation/Alfiürwit.md).

It sits, appropriately, at the "heart" of the Arenfürich rainforest, in the outlands of Raulseách. With growth planes stretching for acres, and some of the most talented cultivators in the corps, the Core produces nearly all of Raulseách's food supply by itself, and ships excess along each of the outgoing rivers.
